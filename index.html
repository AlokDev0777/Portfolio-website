FAQs

How long does it take to build a website?

Most websites are completed within 2–4 weeks depending on scope and timely feedback. Delays in content or feedback may extend the timeline.

What do you need to get started?

Project details, reference websites or design(if any), and content such as text and images.

Do you provide hosting and domain?

I can guide you in purchasing hosting and a domain, or work with your existing setup.

Is post-launch support included?

Yes. Up to 2 months of support is included for bug fixes and minor updates. New features or major changes are handled separately.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Up to 4 revisions are included to refine the project. Additional revisions beyond this may involve extra cost depending on the change

Is website design included?

Design is optional. I can create the design or work with one you provide. Providing your own design reduces overall project scope.

What is your payment structure?

To start the project, a 30% advance is required. The remaining payment is completed after the project is finished and approved.

What if I need additional features later?

Any features outside the initial agreed scope can be added, but they will be treated as additional work with separate cost and timeline.
